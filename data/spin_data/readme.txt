Samples of pre-processed data provided by Yang et. al. in "Self-supervised Learning and Prediction of Microstructure Evolution with Recurrent Neural Networks" [1] in the provided link.

"train.npz" contains 640 sequences at 64x64 resolution for the 10-90 forecasting task.
"valid.npz" contains 159 sequences at 64x64 resolution for the 10-90 forecasting task.
"test.npz" contains 50 sequences at 256x256 resolution for the 10-90 forecasting tastk.

The preprocessed data can be downloaded in the following link: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1lnbPXoo51Nw6bR5cfsgtmPI0rLuEXAsO?usp=sharing

Full data is available for access with the following link: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/xdnjy9p5zn/1 

Citations:
1. K. Yang, Y. Cao, Y. Zhang, M. Tang, F. Zhou, “RNN data”, Mendeley Data, V1, 2021, doi: 10.17632/xdnjy9p5zn.1
